# pl12 history inputs 2020–2025 (decision record 2026-11-06, D2–D3)

## D2 — heat-source replacements in single-family houses

**Count.** NFOŚiGW reports over 603,000 inefficient heat sources replaced under Czyste Powietrze by July 2026
and over 599,000 completed projects by end of May 2026 (`data/observed_2020_2025_poland.md`, block 1).
Estimate for **end of 2025: 550,000** replacements, derived as 603,000 − about 7 months × 4,500 completions
per month in 2026 (≈ 31,000; the May→July difference is not informative because the two figures count
different things) − about 20,000 completions before 2020 (programme start September 2018, slow uptake in
2019). Plausible range 520,000–580,000. Replacements outside the programme (private replacements, municipal
anti-smog programmes, gas-network connections without a grant) are **ignored: the history is a lower bound**
(D2). Sensitivity not run (OPEN O-pl12-1).

**Mix** (cumulative requested sources to 30 June 2026, 908,581 sources, czystepowietrze.gov.pl, D2):
gas condensing 31.2 %, air heat pumps 29.2 %, ground heat pumps 2.5 %, biomass boilers 29.2 %, new coal
boilers 6.4 %, electric 1.2 %, district heat 0.2 %, oil 0.1 %. Applied to the 550,000 completed replacements
(assumes the completed mix equals the requested mix).

**Allocation** (D2): to the four SFH archetypes (all fossil-mix heated: 37 % coal, 34.5 % firewood, 27.5 %
gas, 1 % oil) in proportion to their dwellings, i.e. the same share for each. N_SFH = 5,617,609 dwellings.

| Entry | Share of each SFH archetype's dwellings | Model treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Heat pumps (31.7 %) | 174,350 → **3.104 %** | fixed period-1 adoption of `hvac_fossil_to_hp` (`data/history_heating_pl12.csv`) |
| District heat (0.2 %) | 1,100 → **0.020 %** | fixed period-1 adoption of `hvac_fossil_to_district` |
| New boilers (68.1 %: gas condensing, biomass, class-5 coal, electric, oil) | 374,550 → **6.668 %** | 2025 update of the incumbent fuel mix and efficiency (`fuel_mix_update`, from the first design period) |

**Boiler update.** The replaced old boilers are taken out of the coal and firewood shares in proportion
(heat-pump/DH switchers as well, so the mix describes the dwellings that still have a boiler). New boiler
categories, with the price, CO₂ factor and primary energy factor of their fuel:

| New boiler | Share of replacements | Seasonal efficiency | Fuel price / CO₂ / PEF taken from | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gas condensing | 31.2 % | 0.95 | gas | D2 value; **assumed** (Ecodesign ηs of condensing boilers 0.90–0.94 on data sheets) |
| Modern biomass (pellet) | 29.2 % | 0.87 | firewood price, CO₂ 0, PEF = WOOD_PEF (1.1) | D2 value; brief 2026-11-06 (data sheets: 78–80 % ηs, 88–92 % nominal) |
| Class-5 coal | 6.4 % | 0.85 | coal | D2 value; **assumed** (PN-EN 303-5 class 5 ≥ 88 % nominal) |
| Electric | 1.2 % | 1.00 | electricity price, CO₂ 0 direct, PEF = electricity factor of the period | D2 value |
| Oil | 0.1 % | 0.85 | oil | model's incumbent oil efficiency, **assumed** |

Incumbent efficiencies before: coal 0.70, firewood 0.65, gas 0.87, oil 0.85 (model, certificate methodology).
Resulting SFH incumbent efficiency 0.731 → see `state_after_history.csv` of the history run; the archetype's
delivered base demand is scaled by η_before/η_after so that useful heat is unchanged.

## D3 — envelope renovation 2020–2025

No observed figure (block 3 of the observed-data file). A history run (`pl12_history`) with the heating
history fixed and the Czyste Powietrze rates of the period (0.70 for low-income SFH owner-occupiers, 0.40 for
high-income, all envelope depths and heating; 0 for MFH and landlords) gives the period-1 envelope adoption,
which is then fixed in every pl12 scenario (`data/history_adoption_pl12.csv`). The rates are applied in the
fixed period only; the state's design starts in 2026. History spending is reported separately
(`history_spending.csv`) and is not part of the state's objective (D3/D7).

Assumption recorded: in the history run households anticipate the same rates after 2025 (constant-rate
evaluation); the alternative (rates fall to zero after 2025) was not run (OPEN O-pl12-2).
