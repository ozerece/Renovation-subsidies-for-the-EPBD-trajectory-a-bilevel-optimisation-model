## 5. SQ1 — equity floor and affordability

- **Equity floor (30 % of spending to low-income owners): binding** at the record (30.04 %, strict test). Under the old 0.001 tolerance the search sat at 29.9 % in many scenarios; D10 removed the tolerance and all affected scenarios were re-run. No separate floor-level scenarios (20 % / 40 %) were run in the pl12 family; the pl10 values must not be reused.
- **Cost-neutral affordability rule** (`pl12_afford`): 25,162 M EUR (search bound), +226 M EUR (+0.9 %) against the standard; the standard's own record design does not satisfy the cost-neutral check for every archetype, so the rule binds slightly.
- **Income-share limits** (end state): `s20`–`s40` identical to the standard (24,936 M EUR, same design); **`s10`–`s15` have no feasible design** — the limit is violated at every start although the milestones are met.
- Low-income energy-cost shares with / without the programme: table 3.4.

## 9. Status and caveats for the paper

- The standard value is a **certified local** optimum under the strict floor: no design on the 2.5-pp grid within ±10 pp of the record is cheaper. The search is start-dependent at the 10 % level (27,422 → 24,936 along the proof chain), so a cheaper basin elsewhere is not excluded. Every other scenario is a search bound from 5–6 starts; `envcap_p05`, `hpcost_hi` and `hpcost_lo` are visibly loose.
- Infeasibility is max-σ evidence (every rate at its cap misses a milestone), not a certificate.
- The 2020–2025 envelope history is modelled, not observed (sections 4 and 8).
- The 2030 and 2040 milestones are within one grid step of binding at the record (slack 0.24 and 0.57).
